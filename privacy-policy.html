Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 22, 2026

Welcome to VU Students Guide. This privacy policy describes how we handle information when you use our mobile application. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring a safe experience.

Unofficial Status: Please note that this application is an unofficial educational tool and is not affiliated with the Virtual University of Pakistan.

1. Information Collection and Use

We respect your privacy. We do not collect, store, or share any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as your name, phone number, or email address regarding your use of the app itself.

Log Data & Crash Reports

In the case of an error in the app, we may collect data and information (through third-party products like Google Play Services) on your phone called Log Data to help us fix bugs.

2. LMS Feature and Login Data (WebView)

The app includes a feature that allows users to access the official university Learning Management System (LMS). Please understand the following:

  • WebView Technology: The LMS is displayed using an Android WebView, which acts like a built-in web browser. It simply loads the official university URL.
  • No Password Storage: We do NOT save, record, or store your LMS Student ID or Password on our servers.
  • Direct Interaction: When you log in to the LMS, you are interacting directly with the official university server, not with our app.

3. External Links and Content

Our Service contains links to other sites and resources that are not operated by us. Specifically, this app facilitates access to study materials hosted on:

  • GitHub: Used for repository hosting.
  • Mega.nz: Used for file downloads (past papers, handouts).
  • Official University Website: Accessed via WebView for LMS.

We have no control over and assume no responsibility for the content, privacy policies, or practices of any third-party sites or services.

4. App Permissions

To function correctly, the app may require basic permissions such as:

  • Internet Access: To fetch the latest study materials and load the LMS.
  • Storage/Download Manager: To allow you to save handouts or past papers to your device.

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
